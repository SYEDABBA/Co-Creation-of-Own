# 🚀 OmniSynthNexus (YUGRAAL Invention)

## 🎯 Purpose
An advanced ambient audio workstation and neural brainwave synthesizer designed for deep focus, creative flow, and meditation. It combines multi-layer procedural soundscapes (rain, space drone, fireplace, binaural beats) with a real-time reactive WebGL/Canvas audio visualizer.

## 🔥 Usefulness
Remote professionals, programmers, and students can generate personalized, scientifically tuned soundscapes with custom binaural frequencies (Alpha, Theta, Delta, Gamma) to enhance cognitive performance, eliminate distractions, and track focus sessions with an integrated Pomodoro timer.
